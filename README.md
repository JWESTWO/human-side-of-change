# The human side of AI and change

**Go to the people. Find the real problem. Work it out together.**

A growing collection of practical guides and tools for teams, leaders, people developing their careers, and anyone helping others make change happen.

The resources are free to read and share by linking to this repository. Please respect credited authors and contributors. No general licence to redistribute, modify or commercially reuse third-party material is implied.

## Resources

### Leadership and teams

**[How to run a retrospective where people tell you what they really think](leadership-and-teams/retrospectives/Alex-Okun-Retrospective-Facilitator-Guide.pdf)** (PDF)

A practical facilitator guide with preparation guidance, a suggested run sheet, question choices, ways to handle difficult conversations, and follow-up advice.

**Approach shared by [Alex Okun](https://www.linkedin.com/in/reach-out-to-alex-okun).** The guide identifies additional suggested facilitation tools and examples. Thank you to Alex for sharing the approach and approving this resource for publication.
